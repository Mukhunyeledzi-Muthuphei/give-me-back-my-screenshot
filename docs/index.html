Three steps, once. After that it starts with your Mac and stays out of the way.

    Drag Give Me Back My Screenshot into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it

“Apple could not verify…” on first open? The app is free and isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. Or build it yourself from the source.

Questions

Won't it overwrite whatever I copied?

Yes, that's the point: a new screenshot takes over the clipboard, just as if you'd pressed ⌘C on it. Copied something important? Untick Copy New Screenshots Automatically in the menu to pause it.

I copied something else. How do I get my screenshot back?

Click the menu bar icon and choose Copy Latest Screenshot, or drag the preview at the top of the menu to wherever you want the screenshot.

Why does it need access to my Desktop?

That's where macOS saves screenshots by default, and macOS protects the Desktop folder. If you save screenshots somewhere else (⌘⇧5 → Options), it follows that folder instead.

Does it send my screenshots anywhere?

No. It contains no networking code at all. It reads the new file and hands it to your clipboard. The whole app is one Swift file you can read on GitHub.

Why is there a short delay before it's copied?

While the floating thumbnail is showing, macOS hasn't written the file yet. It lands (and gets copied) when the thumbnail goes away. Swipe the thumbnail off to skip the wait, or turn thumbnails off in ⌘⇧5 → Options.

How do I uninstall it?

Choose Quit from its menu and drag the app from Applications to the Trash.
